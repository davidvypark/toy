<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <what-built>
Complete Vine-style video recording pipeline:
1. CaptureSession - AVFoundation video/audio capture at 720p
2. ClipWriter - H.264 clip writing with proper orientation
3. VideoMerger - Multi-clip merging via AVMutableComposition
4. VideoRecorder - Recording coordinator with 7-second limit
5. CameraPreview - UIViewRepresentable for live camera feed
6. RecordingView - Hold-to-record UI with progress
7. VideoPreviewView - Playback preview with retake/confirm
  </what-built>

  <how-to-verify>
**Test on physical iOS device** (camera does not work in simulator):

1. Build and run the app on your iPhone
2. Sign in (if not already)
3. From HomeView, tap "Record Video" to open RecordingView

**Permission Flow:**
4. Grant camera permission when prompted
5. Grant microphone permission when prompted
6. Verify camera preview appears

**Vine-Style Recording (PART-02):**
7. Press and HOLD the record button
8. Verify: Button shrinks, turns red, progress ring starts filling
9. Release the button
10. Verify: Recording pauses, button returns to normal

**Multi-Clip Recording:**
11. Press and hold again to record more
12. Release to pause
13. Verify: Progress continues from where it left off (cumulative)

**7-Second Limit (PART-03):**
14. Keep recording until 7 seconds total
15. Verify: Recording automatically stops at 7 seconds
16. Verify: State transitions to completed

**Start Over (PART-04):**
17. Before reaching 7 seconds, tap "Start Over"
18. Verify: Progress resets to 0
19. Verify: Can record fresh clips

**Preview (PART-05):**
20. Record clips and tap "Done" (or let it auto-complete at 7s)
21. Verify: Video preview appears
22. Verify: Video plays back (with audio)
23. Tap "Retake"
24. Verify: Returns to recording view, progress reset

**Video Format:**
25. Record and confirm a video
26. Check the output file (via Xcode debug or file inspection)
27. Verify: H.264 codec, 720p resolution, portrait orientation

**Expected Results:**
- Camera permission dialog shows with friendly message
- Mic permission dialog shows with friendly message
- Live camera preview fills screen
- Hold-to-record works smoothly
- Progress ring fills from 0 to 7 seconds
- Time display updates in real-time
- Start Over clears all progress
- Preview shows merged video
- Video plays correctly (not rotated, not mirrored oddly)
  </how-to-verify>
